About Lambo Autos

A trusted name for vehicles across Nigeria

Lambo Autos is a Nigerian automobile company with locations in Abuja and Kaduna, built around a simple idea: finding or moving into a new vehicle should be straightforward, honest and handled by people you can actually reach.

Whether you're buying your first car, trading in your current one, or looking for a vehicle delivered to another state entirely, our team works directly with you — by phone, WhatsApp or email — from first enquiry to delivery.

  • Direct communication — no call centers, a real team on WhatsApp, phone and email
  • Two physical locations in Abuja and Kaduna you can visit
  • Nationwide delivery network covering every Nigerian state
  • Straightforward, honest vehicle assessments for swaps
